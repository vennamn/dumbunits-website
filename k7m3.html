Why This Matters More Than You Think

All small talk starts with the weather.

You spent months learning conjugations and vocabulary. But social connection doesn't happen through perfect grammar—it happens through casual conversation. And in most of the world, that starts with commenting on the temperature.

When you can't instinctively relate to weather chat, you're marked as an outsider. Not because locals judge you, but because you can't participate naturally in the easiest, most universal conversation topic.

You're not just learning a measurement system. You're learning how to belong.

The Real Cost of Always Converting

It's not about the math. It's about the moment.

When someone mentions the temperature and you need to convert:

  • You miss the chance to agree or commiserate naturally
  • You can't make weekend plans based on weather spontaneously
  • You seem distracted (because you are—you're doing math in your head)
  • You feel like a tourist in your own neighborhood

After months of this, you'll eventually learn Celsius organically. But those first months? You'll spend them feeling left out of the most basic conversations.
