Questions?

"How long does it really take?"

Weather temperatures come faster (usually 1-2 weeks of daily play). Cooking temperatures take a bit longer since the range is wider (100-500°F vs 0-100°F for weather)—expect a few weeks of daily practice to feel comfortable across all baking and cooking scenarios.

"Do I need to do all 800+ questions?"

No. The questions repeat common temperatures in different contexts, so you'll see 350°F dozens of times in various recipes. Most people feel confident with everyday temperatures well before seeing every question.

"What if I just want the Temperature Bar reference?"

The app is designed to help you internalize temperatures so you don't need any reference eventually. But yes, you can keep the Temperature Bar visible permanently if you prefer using it as a quick visual guide.

"Is this better than just keeping a conversion chart in my kitchen?"

A static chart doesn't build understanding—you still have to look it up every time. The game gives you repetitions that train your brain to recognize temperatures automatically, the same way you learned Celsius.

"Why do I need the Weather game if I just want to cook?"

You don't—focus on whichever game matters to you. But many cooks find it satisfying to understand both ranges, especially if they watch American films, meet American tourists, or plan to visit the US someday.

"Can I switch between Kitchen and Weather games?"

Yes—play whichever game you want each day. Some people focus on Kitchen until comfortable, then add Weather. Others alternate. Completely up to you.
