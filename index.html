FAQ

Is any math required?

Nope. You learn visually with the Temperature Bar and through real-world context in each question. No conversions, no formulas.

How long does it take to feel confident?

Play for about 30 minutes and you'll already have a feel for the weather forecast. Repeat for a few days and it starts to stick. Kitchen temperatures take a bit longer since the range is wider.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything is on-device. No internet connection needed, no data sent anywhere.

Why is it free?

Because it can be. There's no server to maintain, no data to store, no reason to charge. It's a game that teaches you something useful.

What's the difference between the Weather and Kitchen games?

The Weather Game covers everyday temperatures (roughly 0–100°F) with real travel destinations. The Kitchen Game covers cooking temperatures (100–500°F) with real recipes from around the world.
