FAQ

Is any math required?

Not really. You learn visually via the Temperature Bar and through context-based questions.

How long does it take to feel confident?

Most learners reach basic fluency in weather temperatures within about two weeks just playing a few times per day. Comfort with kitchen temperatures takes slightly longer.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All content is available on-device.
