Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

Introduction

Dumb Units! ("we," "our," or "us") is published by KeepAddIt Works, LLC. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This policy describes our privacy practices.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not require accounts, registration, or any personal data to function.

Local Data Storage Only

  • High Scores: The app stores your 2 highest scores locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.
  • Game Progress: Your current game session is temporarily stored on your device. This data is not transmitted anywhere.

Information We Don't Collect

We do not collect, store, or have access to:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Contact information
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics
  • Crash reports
  • Any form of user tracking

Purchases

If you make an in-app purchase, payment processing is handled entirely by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play Store. We do not receive, process, or store any payment information. Purchase records are managed by the respective app stores under their privacy policies.

Data Sharing

Since we don't collect any data, we have nothing to share, sell, or transfer to third parties. There are no analytics services, advertising networks, or any third-party integrations in our app.

Data Security

Your high scores and game progress remain entirely on your device. The app does not connect to any external servers or services for data storage or processing.

Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for all ages. Since we don't collect any personal information from any users, we don't collect information from children under 13.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app
  • You can reset high scores within the app settings
  • There is no personal data for us to access, modify, or delete because we don't collect any

Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will post the changes on this page with a new effective date. The app will continue to function without collecting personal data.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy:

California Privacy Rights

For California residents: We don't collect personal information, so there is no data to access, delete, or opt out of selling (we don't sell any data).

European Privacy Rights

For EEA residents: Since we don't collect personal data, GDPR data subject rights don't apply. Your gameplay data never leaves your device.
